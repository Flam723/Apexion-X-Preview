# Rev 06A enclosure panel DXFs

These six profiles cover the seven enclosure panels in the [Rev 06A Fusion assembly](../../cad/Apexion-X-Rev-06A.f3d). Both side panels use the same outline. The files are 1:1 in millimetres, with closed outlines, arcs and hole circles. Folder names group the intended materials and `_xN` gives the required quantity.

| Panel | Nominal size (mm) | CAD thickness | Quantity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Side | 514 × 624 | 3 mm smoked PC | 2 |
| Rear | 439 × 624 | 3 mm smoked PC | 1 |
| Top | 439 × 514 | 3 mm smoked PC | 1 |
| Door | 436 × 616.2 | 4 mm PC | 1 |
| Chamber floor | 425 × 500 | 3 mm black ACM | 1 |
| Electronics bottom | 447 × 517.63 | 4 mm 6061 aluminium | 1 |

- **Door and electronics panels.** The CAD calls for 4 mm. Confirm actual stock and clearances before ordering. Mandala Rose Works lists 3 and 6 mm polycarbonate and 3.175 mm 6061 sheet.
- **Chamber floor.** The eight Ø6 mm locations are shown as through-cuts in the DXF, matching the project panel schedule. The Fusion model represents them as blind pockets.
- **Electronics bottom.** The DXF shows twelve Ø3.4 mm and twelve Ø3.2 mm through holes. Specify a 90° countersink to nominal Ø6.4 mm on the bottom face of each Ø3.4 mm hole when the chosen fastener and stock require it. The model shows an approximately Ø6.43 mm mouth. Confirm the stock, face and countersink with the shop.
- **Rear panel.** The DXF includes the cut profile. For an etched logo, specify the decal’s size and location in a separate drawing.

The outlines were taken from the released Fusion solids. The door uses the more relieved face, the floor uses the pocket face, and the electronics panel uses the ordinary clearance-hole face. The six exported profiles were reopened and checked for closed contours, duplicate entities, hole sizes and agreement with the selected CAD faces.
