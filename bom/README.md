# Apexion-X bill of materials

[D3 BOM workbook](D3_Apexion-X_BOM.xlsx) is the v5 design baseline. Review these Rev 06A changes and the [mechanical fit check](../docs/design-reviews/2026-11-19-rev06a-mechanical-fit-check.md) before ordering. The workbook includes the original cut list, printed parts, motion hardware, electronics assumptions, wiring and per-location fastener schedule.

## Rev 06A mechanical changes

| Assembly | Rev 06A fit-up |
| --- | --- |
| Bed and triple-Z | Three WobbleX WS8 stacks using the existing P17–P19 upper dowel seats and the trial Ø3 × 8 mm pins. Use three nominal Ø10 mm M4-threaded steel balls and three Ø12 × 3 mm countersunk disc magnets. Verify the actual Tr8 interfaces, ball thread depth, magnet hot holding force, and hardened contacts. |
| Heater | The 18-position pattern contains ten Ø8.5 mm trial keep-outs. Have the supplier approve the full hole pattern, heater routing and spacer clearance. |
| Upper Z braces | Trial two M5 × 10 button-head screws at P14 and two M5 × 16 at each of P15 and P16. Measure the installed T-nut stacks. |
| Z and A/B motors | Trial twelve M3 × 45 screws with 4 mm spacer stacks at Z, and six M3 × 30 screws with 1.5 mm spacers at A/B. Measure thread and tip clearance on the selected motors. |
| Front idlers | Four rear-entry Ø6.1 × 6.4 mm insert pockets in P11. Confirm the selected inserts and retention. |
| Bed chain | Trial six M3 × 8 countersunk screws at the P19 and P28 chain ends, subject to the selected insert and chain stack. |
| Guides and travel | Verify the selected bearing retention, check Y-rod stiffness and measure loaded and tilted bed travel. |

See Appendix C of the [build manual](../docs/Apexion-X%20Build%20Manual.pdf) for the fastener deltas and fit-up notes. The electronics and wiring tabs are planning inputs. Confirm the installed electronics, protective devices and wiring against the Apexion-X commissioning checks and the [Voron reference setup](https://docs.vorondesign.com/build/electrical/index.html).
