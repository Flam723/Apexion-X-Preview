# TV06+ fixed — 10 mm rods from an SV06 Plus (2026-10-21)

Saved as a new version of "TV06+ fixed". Follows [2026-10-22-round3-fixes](2026-10-22-round3-fixes.md).

## Rods
All 6 linear rods went from Ø8 to Ø10. The SV06 Plus donor set is 6 Ø10 rods: X 2 × 435, Y 2 × 442.5 and Z 2 × 498 mm (partsbuilt.com). Cut them to these lengths:

| Donor rod | Use in TV06+ | Cut to | Cut off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 × 498 (SV06+ Z) | XY rods | 468 | 30 |
| 2 × 442.5 (SV06+ Y) | Front Z | 433 | 9.5 |
| 2 × 435 (SV06+ X) | Rear Z | 429.3 | 5.7 |

The rods are hardened, so cut them with an abrasive disc and chamfer the ends.

## The parts were already 10 mm-ready
- The idler and A/B mount rod bores were already Ø10.3, and the upper Z braces Ø10.0.
- The bed-brace pockets are already LM10UU size (Ø19.2 × 29) with Ø11 rod holes. The XY joints have Ø19.5 pockets.
- Only the motor-mount rod sockets were 8 mm (Ø8.3). They are now Ø10.3: Motor_Mount_Left, Motor_Mount_Right and Motor_Mount_Rear.

## Bores re-centred on the rod axes
The 8 mm rods' slack hid holder offsets of 0.1–1.0 mm. The rods stay on the frame's symmetric nominal axes; the bores and pockets moved inside the printed parts:

| Part | Bore | Move (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| XY_Joint_Left_Lower | Ø19.5 bearing pocket | X −0.65, Z −0.2 |
| XY_Joint_Right_Lower | Ø19.5 bearing pocket | X +0.48, Z −0.31 |
| A/B_Motor_Mount_Upper_Left | Ø10.3 | Z +0.5 |
| A/B_Motor_Mount_Upper_Right | Ø10.3 | X +0.51, Z +0.53 |
| Idler_Body_Left / Idler_Body_Right | Ø10.3 | Z +0.2 |
| Front bed braces | Ø19.2 pocket + Ø11 hole | X ∓0.29, Y +1.0 |
| Rear 3-hole bed brace | both pockets | X +0.68, Y +0.57 |
| Motor_Mount_Left / Motor_Mount_Right | Ø10.3 socket | X ±0.2 |
| Front upper braces | Ø10.0 | Y −0.1 |

Linear_Rod_Upper_Brace_Rear had no rod socket; the rear rod tops just butted into it. It now has Ø10.3 × 2.6 mm sockets.

After the changes, the rods have 0 interference with every holder. A 0.1 mm³ press fit remains in the Ø10.0 upper braces.

**Re-export STLs for:** XY_Joint_Left_Lower, XY_Joint_Right_Lower, both A/B upper mounts, both idler bodies, all three motor mounts, the two front bed braces, the rear 3-hole bed brace and the rear upper brace.

## Bearings (added to the model)
The bearings are in "Printer Hardware (generated)" as idx 507–512.
- **4 × LM10UU** (Ø10 × Ø19 × 29) in the bed-brace pockets: front left, front right and 2 in the rear 3-hole brace. Each fits its 29 mm pocket exactly.
- **2 × LM10LUU** (Ø10 × Ø19 × 55) in the lower XY joints. The designer chose these. Each is seated flush with the back of its 53 mm pocket, so it sticks out 1.94 mm at the front.
- The front entry of each XY pocket was opened through a 0.4 mm lip left over from the re-centring.
- No interference except the bearing bore touching the rod (0.13–0.17 mm³, both Ø10).
