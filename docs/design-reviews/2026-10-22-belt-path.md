# TV06+ fixed — belt path and idlers (2026-10-22)

Both belts are modeled in the component "Belts (generated)", as 6 mm × 1.4 mm solids built from the tangents between the actual pulleys and idlers. Follows [2026-10-22-xy-joint-hardware](2026-10-22-xy-joint-hardware.md).

**Current model state:**
- v12 is the belt-routing version. v13 was the right-idler rework, which the designer rejected ("not going to work").
- v12 was promoted, so it is now v14.

## Routing (standard CoreXY, stacked belts)
| Belt | Level | Path |
|---|---|---|
| A (left motor) | Z 34.5 | L motor pulley → rear-left corner (Ld) → front-left idler → left XY **toothed** idler → toolhead … toolhead → right XY F695 → rear-right corner (Rd) → across the rear → left guide idler (Lp) → L motor |
| B (right motor) | Z 44.5 | the mirror: R motor → Rd → **front-right idler** → right XY toothed idler → toolhead … → left XY F695 → Ld → across the rear → right guide idler (Rp) → R motor |

- **Wrap check:** the wrap directions put the belt teeth on exactly the motor pulleys and the two toothed XY idlers. The smooth back runs on every F695 stack. This matches the modeled parts.
- **Belt length:** 1,865 mm path per belt to the toolhead clamp edges, identical for both. Cut about 1.95 m each to allow for clamping.
- **Belt clamps:** PRODUCT_NAME_2–5 in the Gantry sit where the belts end, so they are the toolhead belt-clamp parts.

## A/B motor mounts (designer correction)
- **Rear corners** (−366, 519.5) and (31, 519.5) carry both belts. Each is a **Ø5 × 30 dowel + a double stack**: a low and a high F695 stack (shim + 2 × F695 + shim each).
  - These were modeled as an M5x10 BHCS + T-nut, now removed.
- **Guide idlers** next to the motors, (−329, 519.5) low and (−6, 519.5) high, are **Ø5 × 25 dowels** pressed into the tapered hole (not M5x25 screws) + an F695 stack.

## Fixes found by the belt path
- **Front idler body screws:** the M5x10 SHCS holding the front idler bodies to the uprights stood 0.7 mm proud of the Ø11 counterbore, into belt A's wrap. Both are now M5x10 BHCS.
- **OPEN: the right front idler is at the wrong level.** It is a left-right copy of the front-left, with its bearing gap at Z 29.4–39.4, but belt B needs Z ≈ 39.5–49.5. *(Resolved later by the BFI symmetric idlers, see [2026-10-22-bfi-symmetric-idlers](2026-10-22-bfi-symmetric-idlers.md).)*
  - Belt B hits Idler_Body_Right (334 mm³), the insert (41 mm³) and a front clamp screw.
  - Flipping the assembly hits the 495RTM rail (1,097 mm³).
- **Rejected approach (do not repeat):** restacking the BFI-style carrier inside the same envelope. That gave a 4.4 mm upper arm under the rail, with the pocket enlarged and the high channel cut into the body. The designer rejected it.

## Research: symmetrical Trident front idlers
- **TV06+'s front idlers are derived from Beefy Front Idlers (BFI) for Trident** (github.com/clee/VoronBFI, GPL-3). The BFI carrier and front are the same size as Idler_Bearing Insert and Idler_Face, and use the same 5 × 18 pin and 2 × F695 + shim stack.
- **Stock Voron Trident** (idler_housing_x2, carrier_a/b_x2, idler_front_x2) and BFI (VT_Housing/Carrier/Front ×2) both use one part set for both sides, with the bearing centred in the carrier. Neither gives a second belt height on its own.
